Our Services

What We Offer

AHCL provides end-to-end energy solutions, from gas processing to distribution, ensuring reliable and efficient energy delivery.

Gas Processing

Advanced natural gas processing and purification technologies for optimal energy extraction and distribution. Our state-of-the-art facilities ensure maximum efficiency and safety.

  • Natural gas purification
  • Gas separation and conditioning
  • Quality control and testing
  • Pipeline distribution

LNG Solutions

Comprehensive liquefied natural gas production, storage, and distribution services. From liquefaction to regasification, we handle the complete LNG value chain.

  • LNG production and liquefaction
  • Cryogenic storage solutions
  • Transportation and logistics
  • Regasification services

Tri-Fuel Terminal

Multi-fuel terminal operations supporting diverse energy distribution networks. Our flexible infrastructure accommodates various fuel types and distribution methods.

  • Multi-fuel storage capabilities
  • Flexible distribution networks
  • 24/7 operations and monitoring
  • Emergency response systems
