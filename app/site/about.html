About AHCL

Leading the energy sector with innovation, expertise, and commitment to excellence

Who We Are

Asiko Hydrocarbon Company Limited (AHCL) is a leading energy company specializing in hydrocarbon solutions. With over 15 years of experience in the industry, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for gas processing, LNG operations, and tri-fuel terminal services.

Our commitment to innovation and sustainable practices has positioned us at the forefront of the energy sector, delivering reliable solutions that power communities and drive economic growth.

Our Vision

To be the leading hydrocarbon company in Africa, recognized for our innovative solutions, operational excellence, and commitment to sustainable energy development.

Our Mission

To deliver sustainable and innovative hydrocarbon solutions that power communities, drive economic growth, and protect our environment for future generations.
